ℹ️ About StudentHub Portal

Charotar University of Science and Technology (CHARUSAT) — Smart Academic Student Portal.

🎯 Our Mission

Our mission is to make learning, academic tracking, and university administrative tasks simple, intuitive, and accessible for every student and faculty member at CHARUSAT.

🌐 What We Do

We provide a centralized digital platform to seamlessly manage attendance, timetables, coursework practicals, fee payments, and verified announcements through a responsive, modern web interface.

✨ Portal Capabilities & Features
📊 Real-Time Analytics

Instant attendance tracking with minimum cutoff warnings and eligibility indicators.

🗓️ Dynamic Scheduling

Organized weekly timetables and midterm/final semester exam schedules.

🌗 Dual Theme Support

High-contrast Light and Dark mode options saved persistently in browser localStorage.

📱 Universal Responsiveness

Built with accessible HTML5, responsive CSS Grid/Flexbox, and mobile-friendly hamburger navigation.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

StudentHub is an online centralized student information portal developed as part of the Web Development Frameworks (WDF) curriculum at CHARUSAT to streamline university communication and academic monitoring.

Faculty members log daily attendance after each lecture or lab session. The system calculates your percentage in real time as (Attended Classes / Total Classes) × 100. A minimum of 80% is required for exam eligibility.

Click the Theme Switcher button (🌙 / ☀️) located in the header of any page or select your choice in the Settings page. Your selection is remembered across browser sessions using HTML5 localStorage.

Access the Exams and Announcements sections directly from your dashboard to view the latest examination schedules, seating arrangements, and syllabus guidelines.

Click on the "Forgot Password" link on the login page or contact the university administration office via the Contact Us page with your Student ID (e.g. 25DCE072).
