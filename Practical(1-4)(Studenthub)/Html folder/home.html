❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about using the StudentHub portal

Faculty members log daily attendance after each lecture or practical session. Your overall percentage is calculated in real time as (Classes Attended / Total Classes) × 100. A minimum of 80% is required for exam eligibility.

Navigate to the Exams section from the top navigation or quick module cards to view subject codes, dates, and morning/afternoon time slots.

Click the Theme Switcher button (🌙 / ☀️) at the top right of the navigation header on any page, or select your preference in the Settings page. Your choice is automatically remembered in your browser's localStorage.

Visit the Fees page to view your total semester dues, current paid balance, and upcoming deadline. Payments can be completed online via NetBanking or UPI.

🎓 StudentHub Portal Quick Guide

Welcome to StudentHub! Here are essential instructions for using your student account:

  • Attendance Rule: 80% minimum attendance is strictly required in all subjects to download exam admit cards.
  • Timetable & Lectures: Check your weekly schedule daily for classroom slots and lab allocations.
  • Fee Payment: Semester dues must be cleared by 30th August 2026. Online receipt is generated instantly.
  • Theme Preference: The portal supports both Light and Dark themes with persistent localStorage memory.

For technical support, reach out to the university IT desk via the Contact Us page.
